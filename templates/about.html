About Chai & Chapter Café

Our Story

Chai & Chapter started in 2019 as a tiny counter with one stove and a shelf of second-hand books. Today it is a Marine Lines neighbourhood favourite for people who want good tea and unhurried conversation.

What We Believe

Slow-brewed, always

We never rush chai. Every pot is simmered with fresh spices, not pre-mixed powders.

Local ingredients

Our tea, milk and spices come from small suppliers across Maharashtra and Assam.

A space to linger

Free Wi-Fi, plenty of plug points and a community book swap on the back wall.

Find Us

12 Marine Lines Road, Mumbai 400002. Open daily, 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
